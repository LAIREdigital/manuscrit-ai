How Manuscrit works

  1. Capture. Tap Dictate and talk, type, or add a screenshot. Pause for a few seconds or say "new note" to start a separate note. Press Ctrl or Cmd + Enter to save.
  2. Plumi sorts. New notes land in the Inbox with a suggested book and chapter. Tap File it, or pick another place. Nothing moves without you.
  3. Teach Plumi. Give each book and chapter a few keywords in Edit book. Add #booktitle to a note to send it straight there.
  4. Shape it. In a chapter, move notes up and down, tick several and Merge them, or tap Read to see the chapter as continuous prose with word counts.
  5. Two modes. Authentique keeps your exact words. Assisté offers edits you approve, and your original is always kept.
  6. Ask Lumière. On a book page, Lumière can summarize chapters, check continuity, find themes and answer questions about your own notes. Needs a free AI key.
  7. Back up. Everything lives in this browser. Download a backup each week, and to move to another device.
